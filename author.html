"Attack on Titan", or "Shingeki no Kyojin" in it's native language, was written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. Isayama was born August 29, 1986, in Ōyama, Ōita Prefecture, Japan. He was attending Hita Rinko Senior High School when he began submitting manga works to contests. He created a 65-page one-shot version of Attack on Titan in 2006. Originally, Isayama offered his work to the Weekly Shōnen Jump department at Shueisha, where the editor of the department asked him to modify a few details in the story and artwork, which he refused. He then brought the manga to the Weekly Shōnen Magazine department at Kodansha, which accepted to support him in publishing, refining and ultimately animating the manga, with the help of Wit Studio, and later on MAPPA. Before serialization began in 2009, Isayama had the ending fully planned out, and had already thought of ideas for plot twists, although they were fleshed out as the series progressed. In 2013, the anime televison series airs, ending it's run in 2023.

...Why,though? Why so grotesque?

He initially based the scenery in the manga on that of his hometown, which is surrounded on all sides by mountains,"as if it is cut off from all civilization", making him feel "so isolated that it was as if I lived on an island. I grew up wondering whether monsters lay beyond the 'wall'" On the topic of inspiration, Isayama spoke of many instances in his daily life, alongside idols he looked up to, that came to shape the series. While working at an internet cafe, Isayama encountered a drunken customer who grabbed him by the collar. It was this incident that showed him "the fear of meeting a person I can't communicate with", which is the feeling that he conveys through the Titans. When designing the appearances of the Titans, he used several models such as martial artist Yushin Okami for Eren Yeager's Titan form, as well as Brock Lesnar for the Armored Titan.
