Attack on Titan: What does it truly mean to be free?

"There's a sea... And just across that sea,freedom's waiting."

What is freedom?

A bird soaring through the skies,above an endless plain of trampled souls?

The boundless sea, merging in the ether of the horizon with the setting sun?

Slaughtering innocent and guilty men alike, in hopes of settling a score long forgotten by both parties?

But who gets to decide if one's guilty or not?

Who gets to play judge in a crude and meaningless war?

Who gets to write the righteous history, and to dictate the justified course of action?

At what point do the opressor and the opressed become one and the same?
